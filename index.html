First-Time Login Guide

Step-by-step onboarding walkthrough for ECE Students & Faculty

1

Select Your Login Section

On the Sign In page, choose your correct section: Division A or Division B (students), or HOD & Faculty Portal. Strict section enforcement connects you to your division's data.

2

Enter Your Official PRN or Email

Enter your permanent college PRN number (e.g., U251H001) or your registered university email address.

3

Use Default Temporary Password

For pre-enrolled students logging in for the first time, your default temporary password is:

Student@123
4

Complete Security Onboarding

Upon signing in, you will be prompted to change your temporary password to a strong personal password and update your profile email to receive notifications and certificate approvals.

5

New Registration / Direct Second Year

If your PRN is not recognized, click Student Sign Up on the login screen to register your details. Once submitted, your registration will be reviewed and approved by the HOD.

Terms & Conditions

Academic ERP Usage Policy • NMIET ECE Department

1. Acceptance of Academic Terms

By accessing and logging into the ECE Campus ERP portal, you agree to adhere strictly to the academic code of conduct and institutional IT policies of Nutan Maharashtra Institute of Engineering & Technology (NMIET), Pune.

2. Authorized Institutional Access Only

This portal is reserved exclusively for enrolled students, verified faculty, and departmental staff of the Electronics & Computer Engineering department. Sharing account credentials, impersonation, or unauthorized access attempts is strictly prohibited.

3. Integrity of Uploads & Certificate Verification

All co-curricular, sports, hackathon, and technical event certificates uploaded for attendance compensation must be genuine and verifiable. Submission of forged, falsified, or tampered certificates is treated as academic malpractice, resulting in permanent cancellation of attendance credits and referral to the College Disciplinary Committee.

4. Coursework, Deadlines & Permanent Deletion

Assignments must be submitted before scheduled deadlines. Submissions are linked securely to authenticated student records. Authorized faculty retain administrative rights to evaluate, score, or permanently delete obsolete assignments and associated submission files.

5. System Security & Non-Abuse

Users must not attempt SQL injection, script tampering, automated scraping, or denial of service against the ERP APIs. Security logs and audit trails are recorded for all transactions and administrative actions.

Revision: Version 2.4 • Effective Academic Year 2025–2026 • Head of Department, ECE (NMIET)

Privacy Policy

How your academic data and personal information are protected

1. Information Collected

We collect and store official student identification details: University PRN, Student Full Name, Roll Number, Division, Batch, Semester, and Institutional Email. Passwords are securely hashed with industry-standard cryptographic salts and are never stored in plain text.

2. Academic Purpose & Processing

Your data is processed exclusively for official college academic activities: certificate verification, attendance compensation processing, timetable management, assignment submissions, and NAAC/NBA departmental reporting.

3. Data Confidentiality & Peer Isolation

Student records are strictly isolated. Other students cannot view, search, or download your personal submissions, uploaded certificates, or contact details. Only authenticated teachers and the Head of Department have role-based administrative access.

4. No Third-Party Commercial Sharing

The Department does not sell, lease, or monetize student or faculty data. No third-party commercial advertising trackers or marketing analytics are deployed on this portal.

5. Profile Corrections & Rights

Students have the right to view their academic records, update their profile settings (including email and password), and request correction of academic details through their Teacher Guardian or HOD.

Data Protection Officer: NMIET Department of ECE • Pune, Maharashtra
